# Food tables

Nutrition per 100 g (per 100 ml for drinks): kcal, and protein, carbohydrate and
fat in grams. Meat and fish are left out. An empty value was not measured.

| File | Holds | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `cofid.csv` | Generic foods and ingredients | CoFID 2021 |
| `tesco.csv` | Tesco products sold in the UK, from their labels | Open Food Facts |
